SFX level check

Decodes every cue in assets/audio/sfx/manifest.json and works out what playback gain each needs to sit at its intended loudness. The generated files land anywhere from −27 dB to −3 dB depending on what the model felt like, so these numbers are what keep a boss slam from being twenty decibels louder than a star.

Serve the repo (python3 -m http.server 8000) and open /tools/measure-sfx.html — file:// won't work, the page fetches the audio. Paste the generated block into the S table in scripts/battle/util.js. Re-run this after regenerating any cue.
